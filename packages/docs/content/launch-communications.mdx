# Mainnet Launch Communications Evidence

**Status:** Pending | **Owner:** Community lead

Before launch, replace the pending
[communications evidence record on GitHub](https://github.com/Invoice-Liquidity-Network/Invoice-Liquidity-Network/blob/dev/docs/evidence/launch-communications.md)
with reproducible evidence of the approved announcement, user support
channels, incident contact, and maintainer availability plan. Link the
published announcement and approved support and incident runbook. A pending
record is not launch approval.

The
[mainnet launch evidence workflow](https://github.com/Invoice-Liquidity-Network/Invoice-Liquidity-Network/blob/dev/.github/workflows/mainnet-launch-evidence.yml)
and
[readiness checklist](https://github.com/Invoice-Liquidity-Network/Invoice-Liquidity-Network/blob/main/docs/mainnet-launch-checklist.md)
remain authoritative for launch gating. The Release lead owns recovery of
readiness evidence; the Community lead owns completion of this record.
